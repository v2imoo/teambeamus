Team Experiences · Rhythm & music

Rhythm & music gets a room in sync.

Drumming and music-making that pull a whole group into time together — within minutes, with no skill required.

Talk to usAll team experiences
What it is

Sync you can hear.

There is something almost unfair about rhythm: put instruments in a few hundred hands and, within minutes, a room that arrived as strangers is playing as one. It is the fastest, most literal way to make a large group feel aligned — and it needs zero musical skill to work.

It is also genuinely inclusive. Everyone can keep a beat, so nobody is left on the sidelines, which makes it a rare activity that works across every kind of team.

What’s inside

Ways to make sound together.

Drum circles

Music-making

Building a piece together from scratch, part by part.

Body percussion

Finale performances

Common questions

About Rhythm & music

Do we need musical ability?

None at all. The whole point is that anyone can do it, and a room of beginners sounds remarkable within minutes.

How big can it be?

From a team to a full auditorium. It is one of the few activities that gets more powerful the larger it gets.
