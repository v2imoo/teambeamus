Who it’s for · The annual retreat

A retreat that isn’t exhausting.

For the once-a-year gathering that too often crams so much in that people come back needing a rest from the retreat.

Talk to usWho it’s for
The pressure here

The retreat that wears people out.

The annual retreat carries a lot of hope and a packed agenda — and often sends people home more tired than they arrived, having done everything except actually connect. A good retreat has an arc and real downtime, not a wall-to-wall schedule.

We design the whole thing around a goal, with genuine space built in, so people leave closer rather than depleted. And we measure it, so the biggest team investment of the year is one you can prove worked.

What fits an annual retreat.

How do we avoid overloading the agenda?

By designing around a goal and protecting downtime as part of the plan. Less, done well, beats more, done frantically.

Can you run the whole thing?

Yes — from sourcing the venue to running the days to measuring the outcome.
