Where we go · India

Shimla & Manali for teams.

Mountains and pine · fly to Chandigarh, then drive. A proper change of scene, high in the Himalayas.

Plan a trip hereAll destinations
Why Shimla & Manali

The mountains reset everyone.

Shimla and Manali are the real mountains — pine forests, snow lines and clean, thin air. The scale of the Himalayas puts a team’s day-to-day into perspective, which is often exactly what a stuck group needs.

They suit longer retreats where the journey is part of the experience, and teams that want adventure and grandeur together.

Getting there & when to go

The practical bit.

Getting there

Fly to Chandigarh and drive up (a few hours to Shimla, longer to Manali), or take the scenic route by road from Delhi.

When to go

March to June and September to November are most reliable. Winter brings snow and beauty but harder travel.

How long

Most teams come for one to three days. We will tell you honestly what a given goal needs, and match the length to it.

Common questions

About Shimla & Manali

How much travel is involved?

A fair amount — plan for the drive up as part of the trip. It rewards teams who come for three days or more.

Shimla or Manali?

Shimla is more accessible and genteel; Manali is wilder and more adventurous. We will match it to your group.
