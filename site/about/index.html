About

One business. Two homes. One idea.

We design, deliver and measure corporate team experiences — in India and worldwide, with a home in the USA at teambeam.us.

Talk to usHow we work

We built TeamBeam because most team events are forgotten by Friday, and nobody can say if they helped. We wanted to do it the other way around — understand the team first, build the day around a real goal, and come back afterwards to see what changed.

What makes us us

A few things we do differently.

Diagnostic-first

We read the team before we design anything, so the day fits the real gap.

Measured

We check at Day 14, 30 and 60, so you get proof rather than a memory.

No forced fun

Calm, real and built for grown-ups. Every minute has a point.

One team worldwide

India or the USA, the same practice and the same people, wherever you gather.

“We build teams. And we prove it.” That second sentence is the whole company.
How we got here

We were tired of days that felt good and changed nothing.

TeamBeam grew out of a simple frustration: teams spend real money on events that are forgotten by Friday, and nobody can say whether they helped. We wanted to run the other experiment — understand the team, design for a real goal, and measure what actually changed.

Today we do that as one business with two homes: teambeam.in for India and the world, and teambeam.us for the United States. Same method, same standard, wherever your team sits.
