Impact & CSR · Beam Builds

Build something a community needs.

A team builds or assembles something a community genuinely needs, in a single focused day — bonding and giving back in the same breath.

Talk to usAll Impact & CSR
What it is

The best build serves someone.

There is a particular satisfaction in building something with your team and then handing it to someone who needed it. Beam Builds channels that — assembling bikes or wheelchairs, constructing a facility, putting together equipment — into a day that bonds the team through the work and gives back through the result.

It is give-back and team-building at once, and like all our CSR work, it comes with a report you can file.

About Beam Builds

Where does what we build go?

To a matched cause or community partner. We arrange the recipient and handle logistics, and you get a report on the impact.

How many can take part?

From a single team to a large group split into build stations. We scale the project to the number.
